007FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

Is there a minimum order volume?

No. There is no minimum volume, no onboarding fee and no long contract. You fund your account before stock arrives and the balance draws down as orders go out — that is the only commitment we ask for.

How is storage charged?

£0.30 per cubic foot per month, on the space your stock actually occupies, from the day it lands. There is a £0.05 per-unit minimum that applies only where the volume calculation falls below it — you pay whichever is higher, never both. The rate is the same all year, including Q4.

Do you mark up postage?

No. Postage is passed straight through at Royal Mail's published online rate. Postage is VAT-exempt and recharged at cost; VAT applies only to our own fulfilment and storage charges.

Which couriers do you use?

Royal Mail and DPD, through our own established accounts. If you'd rather buy your own labels, you can — or we'll generate them for £0.10 per label.

When are orders dispatched?

Standard orders are dispatched the same working day.

Can you pack with my own branded packaging?

Yes. If you supply your own boxes, mailers or inserts, we'll pick, pack and label with them.

What about items bigger than a Medium Parcel?

Tell us the dimensions and weight and we'll price it for you. Use the quote form and choose "Larger than Medium".

How quickly can I get started?

Most sellers are live within a week of their stock landing at our warehouse.

Tell us what you're shipping.

We'll tell you what it costs. No sales process.
