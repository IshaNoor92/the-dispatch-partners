Privacy policy

How The Dispatch Partners Ltd uses the information you give us.

On this page 01What we collect 02Why we use it 03Who processes it 04How long we keep it 05Your rights 06Cookies

Who we are. The Dispatch Partners Ltd, 2 Lovell Walk, Rainham, RM13 7ND, registered in England & Wales, is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Contact us at contact@thedispatchpartners.com.

What we collect

When you use our quote form we collect the details you enter: your name, business name, email address, phone number, website, and information about the products you sell and ship.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you pricing (legitimate interests / steps prior to a contract).
  • To set up and run your account if you become a customer (performance of a contract).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who processes it

Form submissions are received through our website host, Netlify, Inc., which processes them on our behalf. Our contact page embeds a Google Maps view, which is provided by Google.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an account are deleted within 12 months. Customer records are kept for as long as UK tax and company law requires.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies.
